    How to read it

    • Time runs outward. The centre is the deep past, the outer ring is today.
    • A line is one language, spanning its lifetime. Gaps mark a new stage, like Old → Middle English.
    • Dotted lines are reconstructed proto-languages, never written down.
    • Colour is family. The outer band names it. Zoom in for more labels.

    Families

      Arrow keys move through the tree: left and right between sibling languages, up to the parent, down to the first descendant. Plus and minus zoom, zero shows the whole tree, Escape clears the selection.
